# Terminology

This glossary defines project terms. Normative specifications may further narrow these meanings.

## Claim
A statement about a subject, process, event, condition, or outcome that can in principle be supported or challenged by evidence.

## Evidence
A record relevant to evaluating a claim. Evidence may include structured events, documents, sensor readings, images, video references, inspection results, or signed attestations.

## Evidence provenance
Information describing where evidence came from, who or what created it, and relevant custody or transformation history.

## Evidence integrity
The degree to which unauthorized or undisclosed modification of a digital record can be detected.

## Verification
A documented process that checks some aspect of a claim, evidence set, identity, integrity property, or conformance requirement.

Verification does not necessarily mean independent verification.

## Independent verification
Verification by a party sufficiently independent from the subject and relevant commercial interest for the stated purpose, under a disclosed method.

## Conformance
Satisfaction of a defined Trust & Life requirement or profile within a stated scope and time period.

Conformance is preferred over broad claims such as “certified safe” unless a legally and institutionally appropriate certification scheme actually exists.

## Attestation
A statement issued by an identifiable party about a subject, observation, result, or verification decision.

## Subject
The entity to which a claim or evidence record refers, such as an organization, farm, factory, facility, product, batch, shipment, or process.

## Supply-chain event
A time- and context-bound record of something relevant that occurred to or around products or other supply-chain objects.

## Process transparency
Proportionate visibility into processes relevant to public or supply-chain trust claims.

## Continuous observability
A condition in which a defined process is covered by recurring or continuous evidence under documented rules, including disclosure of material gaps and outages.

It does not imply that every physical angle or every person is continuously recorded.

## Public review
A structured mechanism allowing members of the public or other stakeholders to submit evidence-backed questions or reports for review.

## Incident
A reported or verified condition that may conflict with a requirement, expected process, safety rule, or trust claim.

## Evidence coverage
The proportion or scope of the relevant process for which required evidence is available. Coverage may be temporal, spatial, event-based, or requirement-based.

## Marketplace
Any transactional interface that lists or sells products. A marketplace is an optional implementation of Trust & Life and is not the protocol itself.

## Registry
A service or dataset that publishes identities, profiles, conformance states, attestations, or related metadata. Multiple independent registries may implement the protocol.
