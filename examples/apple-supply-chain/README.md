# Demo Apple Supply Chain

This is the canonical v0.1 Trust & Life example.

It demonstrates:

- a producer organization and logistics provider;
- a batch and linked shipment;
- T1 evidence-backed production records;
- T2 continuous-observation and sensor claims;
- a T3 claim with an independent verifier;
- evidence integrity metadata;
- an explicit empty incident list;
- a profile conformance statement with limitations.

All organizations, records, hashes, reports, and observations are synthetic placeholders. The example MUST NOT be presented as evidence about a real organization or food product.
