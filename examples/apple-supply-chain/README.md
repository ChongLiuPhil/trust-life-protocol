# Demo Apple Supply Chain — v0.2

This is the canonical synthetic Trust & Life example.

It demonstrates:

- a producer, logistics provider, laboratory, and registry/verifier;
- a batch and linked shipment;
- T1 evidence-backed production records;
- T2 continuous-observation and sensor claims;
- a T3 laboratory claim with an independent verifier;
- real SHA-256 digests over the demonstration evidence files;
- a mixed-level conformance statement whose overall level is conservatively aggregated at T1;
- a W3C VC 2.0 data-model fixture for the laboratory attestation;
- an illustrative GS1 EPCIS export under `epcis/`;
- an explicit empty incident list.

All organizations, identifiers, reports, events, observations, and credentials are synthetic. The example MUST NOT be presented as evidence about a real organization or food product.
